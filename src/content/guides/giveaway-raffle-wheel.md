## Why use a wheel for a giveaway

People who enter giveaways want to know the winner wasn't chosen in advance. Picking a name off a list behind the scenes can't show that, but a spinning wheel on camera can. The audience watches the same random process you do, and the result is final the moment the wheel stops.

## Before you spin

1. **Close the entry window.** Write down the exact cutoff time and stick to it.
2. **Clean the list.** Remove duplicates that break your rules, accounts that didn't meet the entry requirements, and anyone who is ineligible.
3. **Count the entries.** Note the total so viewers can match it to the slices on the wheel.
4. **Decide on backup winners.** If the first winner doesn't respond within a set time, a new spin picks the next one. Say how long they have before you draw.

## Running the draw

Open the wheel, paste in the entries, and start a screen recording before you touch anything. Show the full list, spin once, and announce the result immediately. If you are picking several winners, remove each winner after their spin so nobody wins twice.

## After the draw

Post the recording alongside the announcement, contact the winner privately, and keep the entry list for a few weeks in case anyone asks questions. That small amount of record keeping builds trust for your next giveaway.

## A note on rules

Many places treat prize draws differently from raffles that require payment, and platforms like Instagram and TikTok have their own promotion guidelines. For anything beyond a small prize, read your platform's terms and local rules before you launch.
