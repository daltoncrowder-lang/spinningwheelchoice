## Why a wheel helps couples

The hard part of date night usually isn't the date. It's the planning, and the back-and-forth of "what do you want to do?" A wheel turns planning into a ten-second spin, and it adds a little surprise that a routine plan doesn't have.

## Set it up together

Sit down once and build your list. Each person adds ideas, and each can veto anything they really don't want to do. Agree that once something is on the wheel, you'll do it if it comes up. That one rule is what makes the wheel work.

## At-home ideas

- Cook a recipe from a country neither of you has visited.
- Build a blanket fort and watch a movie from the year you met.
- Have a themed dinner where you dress for the theme.
- Play a two-player board game or card game tournament.
- Do a puzzle with music and snacks.

## Going-out ideas

- Try a restaurant neither of you has heard of.
- Visit a local farmers market and cook dinner from what you buy.
- Take a short drive somewhere new and find a coffee shop.
- Go bowling, mini golf, or to an arcade.
- Catch live music at a small venue.

## Low-budget ideas

- A sunset walk with a thermos of something hot.
- Stargazing from the darkest spot nearby.
- A picnic in a park.
- A "two-dollar date" where you each spend two dollars on a surprise for the other.

Keep a separate wheel for each category, so you can pick the wheel that fits the night and still let the spin choose the details.
